AI, Math and Machine Proof

Here is a collection of links on AI, math and machine proof, triggered by the current ongoing competition by big GenAI companies to finish off math.

Machine proof is key since GenAI produced informal mathematics cannot be trusted before it has been formalized, made checkable by GOFAI (symbolic AI) tools.

Results obtained by AI/machine proof historically

  • 1976: Proof of four-color theorem by Kenneth Appel and Wolfgang Haken with machine assistance,
    2005: full formalization in Coq by Georges Gonthier
  • 1998: Proof of Kepler conjecture by Thomas Hales with Samuel Ferguson with machine assistance,
    2014: full formalization in Isabelle/HOL Light by Thomas Hales and colleagues,
    2017: proof finally published
